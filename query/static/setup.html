Connect your dashcam

  1. 1

    Connect your dashcam once

    Pair your dashcam (or phone) with Kerb Map one time. After that there is nothing to do.

  2. 2

    It syncs on its own

    As you drive, every clip uploads to VAST automatically and is processed there with NVIDIA Cosmos: parked cars and free kerb are read from the video and added to the live map.

  3. 3

    Add a clip now (optional)

    Send a recorded clip yourself, or try a sample, to watch it being processed.
