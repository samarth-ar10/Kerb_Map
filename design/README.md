# design/

Prototype material for the product design and slides.

- `build-data.py`: builds a compact `data.js` for design mock-ups from the Toronto drive GPS and the street-camera
  stills in the designer pack (not in this repo). **Prototype data only:** its `ago` column is a placeholder made from
  drive order, not a real "last seen" time, and states are unlabelled. Nothing here feeds the app; the app reads real
  receipts from the kerbmap tables.
